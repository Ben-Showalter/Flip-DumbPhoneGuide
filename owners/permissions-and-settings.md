# Permissions and settings

Many apps need you to switch something on before they can do their job: use the microphone,
read files, show notifications on the home screen, and so on. Good apps show a checklist (✓ or
✗ for each item) and a button that takes you to the right place.

## A simple permission (microphone, contacts, location…)
1. The app asks: "Allow *app* to…?"
2. Highlight **Allow** and press **OK**.

If you pressed **Deny** by mistake:
1. Go to **Settings** › **Apps** › the app › **Permissions**.
2. Highlight the permission (for example **Microphone**), press **OK**, and choose **Allow**.

> The first time you hold a voice-typing key, the microphone question uses up that press.
> Press the key again after allowing it.

## Accessibility (for apps that react to keys or read the screen)
Some apps need an **accessibility service** to catch a key anywhere or to show notifications
on the home screen.
1. In the app, choose its accessibility button, or go to **Settings** › **Accessibility**.
2. Highlight the app's service and press **OK**.
3. On the switch screen: press **Down**, then **Up** (to land on the switch), then **OK**.
4. A warning appears. Press **Down** to the **OK** button and press **OK**.

- **On the Kyocera E4811 this doesn't work:** the switch won't stay on. Use a computer instead;
  see [Using a computer](computer-setup.md#turn-on-an-apps-accessibility-service).
- **After an app update**, if the app stops reacting to keys, switch its service **off and on
  once**.
- On newer Android versions (13+), the switch may be greyed out for apps you installed
  yourself. Go to **Settings** › **Apps** › the app, press **Options** (or the **⋮** menu), and
  choose **Allow restricted settings** *(may differ)*, then try again.

## Display over other apps
Lets an app show a small status message on top of other apps.
1. Go to **Settings** › **Apps** › **Default apps** *(may differ)*.
2. Go back one level to **Special app access** › **Display over other apps**.
3. Highlight the app and press **OK**, then press **Up**, then **Down** to the switch, and
   **OK** so it says **On**.

## All files access (Android 11 and newer)
1. Go to **Settings** › **Apps** › **Special app access** › **All files access**.
2. Highlight the app, press **OK**, highlight **Allow access to manage all files**, press **OK**.
3. Press **Back**.

On Android 10 and older (the DuraXV phones), just choose **Allow** when the app asks for storage.

## Usage access
Some apps need it to know which app is on screen.
1. Go to **Settings** › **Apps** › **Special app access** › **Usage access**.
2. Highlight the app, press **OK**, and turn it **On**.

## Battery: "Run in the background"
Apps that must keep running (messages, calls) need to be left alone by battery saving.
1. When the app asks, or in **Settings** › **Apps** › **Special app access** › **Battery
   optimization** *(may differ)*,
2. Choose **All apps**, highlight the app, press **OK**, choose **Don't optimize**, then
   **Back**.

## Default apps
- **Default texting app:** when an app asks "Change SMS app?", choose **Yes**.
- **Default home screen:** when the phone asks which Home app to use, highlight the one you
  want and choose **Always**. To change it back: **Settings** › **Apps** › **Default apps** ›
  **Home app**.

## "Send many messages?"
If you text a long list, Android stops after about 30 messages and asks whether the app may
send this many. Choose to **always allow**.

## Notification access on the DuraXV (E4810 / E4811)
On these phones, **Notification access** looks switched on but never works for apps you
installed yourself. Apps that need your notifications use their **accessibility** option
instead, so turn that on (above).
