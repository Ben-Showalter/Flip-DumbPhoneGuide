# Notes for each phone

Technical details for developers are in [../docs/devices.md](../docs/devices.md).

## Kyocera DuraXV Extreme (E4810)
- Android 9. Small 2.6-inch screen plus an outside screen for alerts.
- **Options** is the right soft key. **Clear** works as **Back**.
- **Notification access** doesn't work for apps you installed; use their accessibility option.
- Holding the right soft key may not register as a hold.

## Kyocera DuraXV Extreme (E4811)
- Android 10.
- **Accessibility services can't be switched on from Settings**: use a computer
  ([how](computer-setup.md#turn-on-an-apps-accessibility-service)).
- **Notification access** doesn't work for apps you installed.
- Some apps can't read files in their own folder on this Android version until their maker
  fixes it.

## Kyocera DuraXE Epic (E4610)
- Android 7. Wide 320×240 screen.
- Some map apps that use 3D graphics crash on it; apps that draw maps simply work.
- Large text-message histories load slowly.

## Sonim XP-series (XP3800, XP5700…)
- **Options** is the **Menu** key; apps show the word "Options" on the left after asking once.
- The XP5700 runs Android 5.1, so some newer apps won't install.
